    **EPA/DHA heart claim — Reg 432/2012**

    Permitted wording: "EPA and DHA contribute to the normal function of the
  heart" [Reg 432/2012, Annex (Docosahexaenoic acid (DHA); Eicosapentaenoic acid
  and docosahexaenoic acid (EPA/DHA))].

    Conditions of use: "The claim may be used only for food which is at least a
  source of EPA and DHA as referred to in the claim SOURCE OF OMEGA-3 FATTY
  ACIDS as listed in the Annex to Regulation (EC) No 1924/2006. In order to bear
  the claim information shall be given to the consumer that the beneficial
  effect is obtained with a daily intake of 250 mg of EPA and DHA." [Reg
  432/2012, Annex (Docosahexaenoic acid (DHA); Eicosapentaenoic acid and
  docosahexaenoic acid (EPA/DHA))]. No restriction/warning column entry was
  returned for this claim [Reg 432/2012, Annex (Docosahexaenoic acid (DHA);
  Eicosapentaenoic acid and docosahexaenoic acid (EPA/DHA))]. General rules
  still apply: claims must refer to the food ready for consumption and the
  substance must be present in a significant quantity in the amount reasonably
  expected to be consumed [Reg 1924/2006, Article 5].

⏺ EPA and DHA — "contribute to the normal function of the heart" (Reg. 432/2012,
  Annex)

  Two conditions must both be met:

  1. Composition threshold — the food must be at least a "source of EPA and 
     DHA", i.e. meet the nutrition claim SOURCE OF OMEGA-3 FATTY ACIDS in the
     Annex to Reg. 1924/2006.
  2. Mandatory consumer information — the label must tell the consumer that the 
     beneficial effect is obtained with a daily intake of 250 mg of EPA and DHA.

  No warning or restriction-of-use entry attaches to this claim. The general
  rules of Reg. 1924/2006 Art. 5 still apply: the claim refers to the food as 
  consumed, and the substance must be present in a significant quantity in the
  amount reasonably expected to be eaten.

  Related DHA claims (distinct entries, don't mix the thresholds): "DHA 
  contributes to maintenance of normal brain function" and "...normal vision"
  require ≥ 40 mg DHA per 100 g and per 100 kcal, plus the same 250 mg/day
  consumer information — note it's 250 mg of DHA alone there, not EPA+DHA.
