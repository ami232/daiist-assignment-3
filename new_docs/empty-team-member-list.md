# Why is the list empty when I assign an estimate to a producer?

When you assign an estimate (the **Assign** icon on the estimate row, or **Edit Budget → Document fields → Assigned to**), the **Team member** list shows only the active members of your company. If it is empty, the person you want is not on your team yet. Chedar has no separate "producer" field: you assign the estimate to a team member.

## Why the list is empty

The most common reasons, most likely first:

1. **Nobody has been added to the team yet.** The list only shows people listed under **Settings → Team**.
2. **The producer works with you as a freelancer.** Freelancers have access to specific projects only and never appear in this list.
3. **The person was removed or deactivated.** Only active members are listed. People who were invited but have not set a password yet do appear.
4. **You are signed in as a freelancer.** Freelancers cannot see the company's team list.

The list is the same for every estimate in the company. It does not depend on the project or the budget template.

## Add the person to your team

You need to be a Company admin to do this.

1. Go to **Settings → Team**.
2. Click **+ Member**.
3. Fill in **First name**, **Last name**, **Email** and **Role**. For a producer, pick **Member** or **Production manager**.
4. Save. The person gets an invite email and appears in the team list straight away.

If the person currently works with you as a freelancer, Chedar asks you to remove their project access first, then add them to the team. Members take a paid seat, except Viewers and Accountants.

## Assign the estimate

You need the Production manager role or above, and the estimate must not be finalized.

1. In **All estimates**, click the **Assign** icon on the estimate's row.
2. Pick the person under **Team member**.
3. Click **Save**.

Tip: in **Settings → Workflow**, turn on **Assign new estimates to the person who creates them** to fill this in automatically for new estimates.
