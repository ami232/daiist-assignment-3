## Watch list rule

Demo Bank places a retail obligor on its watch list when the obligor is more than 15 days past due on any credit obligation. The credit monitoring team reviews every watch-list obligor within 10 working days and reports the list to the risk committee each month.

## Removal

An obligor leaves the watch list after 3 consecutive months without any past-due amount.
