**Agent's answer:**

> To obtain the A2 remote pilot certificate, you must complete the following steps in order [Reg 2019/947, UAS.OPEN.030]:
>
> 1.  **Complete online training and pass the theoretical examination** for subcategory A1/A3 [Reg 2019/947, UAS.OPEN.030].
> 2.  **Complete self-practical training** in the operating conditions of subcategory A3 [Reg 2019/947, UAS.OPEN.030].
> 3.  **Declare the completion** of the self-practical training and **pass an additional theoretical knowledge examination** (at least 30 multiple-choice questions) provided by the competent authority or a designated entity [Reg 2019/947, UAS.OPEN.030].
>
> ### Practical Skills to Train
> The goal of the self-practical training is to ensure you can operate a C2 class UAS smoothly, accurately, and safely [EASA AMC-GM Part-UAS, AMC1 UAS.OPEN.030(2)(b)]. You should perform as many flights as necessary to gain proficiency in the following [EASA AMC-GM Part-UAS, AMC2 UAS.OPEN.030(2)(b)]:
>
> *   **Preparation:** Verify payload compatibility, ensure the operation zone is suitable and meets technical requirements, define the area of operation, and identify local geographical zone limitations (e.g., no-fly zones) [EASA AMC-GM Part-UAS, AMC2 UAS.OPEN.030(2)(b)].
> *   **Flight Manoeuvres:** Practice take-off/launch, landing/recovery, precision flight manoeuvres within a defined airspace, hovering in all orientations, and loitering [EASA AMC-GM Part-UAS, AMC1 UAS.OPEN.030(2)(b)].
> *   **Abnormal Situations:** Exercise procedures for abnormal situations (e.g., return-to-home) as specified in the manufacturer’s manual [EASA AMC-GM Part-UAS, AMC1 UAS.OPEN.030(2)(b)].
> *   **Control Schemes:** If your UAS has both manual and automated control schemes or multiple automated features, you must demonstrate proficiency in all of them [EASA AMC-GM Part-UAS, AMC1 UAS.OPEN.030(2)(b)].
>
> The training should be conducted with a UAS that has similar flight characteristics, control schemes, and weight (MTOM < 4 kg) to the one you intend to use [EASA AMC-GM Part-UAS, AMC1 UAS.OPEN.030(2)(b)].
