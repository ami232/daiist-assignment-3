**Pregunta:** How is an excessive anticoagulant effect from a warfarin overdose reversed?

**Referencia:** Stop warfarin and, if necessary, give oral or parenteral vitamin K1; prothrombin complex concentrate, fresh frozen plasma or activated factor VII may be considered when reversal is urgent.

**Respuesta del agente:**

> Treatment for excessive anticoagulation is based on the INR level, the presence of bleeding, and clinical circumstances [warfarin:overdosage:1]. Reversal may be obtained by discontinuing warfarin therapy and, if necessary, administering oral or parenteral vitamin K1 [warfarin:overdosage:1]. If urgent reversal is required, prothrombin complex concentrate (PCC), fresh frozen plasma, or activated Factor VII may be considered, though these are associated with risks of thrombosis and viral disease [warfarin:overdosage:1]. These blood products should be reserved for exceptional or life-threatening bleeding episodes [warfarin:overdosage:2]. Please consult the prescriber or pharmacist for specific clinical management.

## q11 · single

**Pregunta:** How much grapefruit juice is too much for a patient on atorvastatin?

**Referencia:** More than 1.2 liters of grapefruit juice daily is not recommended.

**Respuesta del agente:**
