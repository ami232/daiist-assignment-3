**Reference:** Only if the KID is misleading, inaccurate or inconsistent with binding documents or Article 8; damages are claimed under national law.  
**Expected:** PRIIPs | Article 11

**Agent answer:**

> Yes, a retail investor can claim damages from a PRIIP manufacturer if they demonstrate a loss resulting from their reliance on a Key Information Document (KID) that is misleading, inaccurate, or inconsistent with the relevant parts of legally binding pre-contractual and contractual documents or with the requirements laid down in Article 8 of the PRIIPs Regulation [PRIIPs | Article 11, p. 14].
> 
> Such claims are made in accordance with applicable national law [PRIIPs | Article 11, p. 14]. Additionally, PRIIP manufacturers are required to establish procedures to ensure that retail investors have an effective way to submit complaints and access redress procedures [PRIIPs | Article 19, p. 18].
