## After

No, the airline cannot deny you boarding on the grounds that you used your own printed version of a digitally issued boarding pass, provided it is printed in a clear and machine-readable way [reg_2026_2202, Article 1]. Furthermore, airlines cannot charge you an additional fee or extra charge for using your own printed version of a digitally issued boarding pass [reg_2026_2202, Article 1].
