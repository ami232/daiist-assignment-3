## After

The *Bono Alquiler Joven* provides a direct subsidy of **250 euros per month** for up to two years (a total of 6,000 euros across 24 monthly payments), limited to the monthly rent or price of the rental or cession agreement [BOE-A-2022-802, Artículo 11, Preámbulo]. It is aimed at young people up to 35 years of age inclusive with limited financial means whose household income does not exceed 3 times the IPREM [BOE-A-2022-802, Preámbulo].

Regarding how to apply, the management, processing, and resolution of the subsidy correspond entirely to the competent bodies of your **autonomous community** or the cities of Ceuta and Melilla [BOE-A-2022-802, Artículo 4]. They are responsible for issuing the continuous and permanent public calls for applications and handling the procedures [BOE-A-2022-802, Artículo 13]. (Note: This state regulation does not apply in the Basque Country and Navarra, which manage their own schemes) [BOE-A-2022-802, Artículo 2]. To apply, you must submit your application directly through the official housing channels or electronic office of the autonomous community where your rental home is located.
