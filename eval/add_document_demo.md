## After

In U-space airspace, U-space service providers must provide UAS operators with the following mandatory services [reg_2021_664_u-space.html, Article 3]:

*   The network identification service referred to in Article 8 [reg_2021_664_u-space.html, Article 3].
*   The geo-awareness service referred to in Article 9 [reg_2021_664_u-space.html, Article 3].
*   The UAS flight authorisation service referred to in Article 10 [reg_2021_664_u-space.html, Article 3].
*   The traffic information service referred to in Article 11 [reg_2021_664_u-space.html, Article 3].

U-space service providers are responsible for providing these services during all phases of operations in that U-space airspace [reg_2021_664_u-space.html, Article 7]. Additionally, based on an airspace risk assessment, Member States may require additional U-space services selected from those referred to in Articles 12 and 13 [reg_2021_664_u-space.html, Article 3].
