## After

I can't find the answer in the corpus laws (LAU, LDV, LPH). The details on how to apply for the Bono Alquiler Joven are not contained in the urban rental, housing rights, or horizontal property laws of the corpus, but rather in separate regulations (such as Real Decreto 42/2022) and the specific application procedures of each autonomous community; please consult your autonomous community's housing department, an *abogado*, or the OCU for guidance.

Regarding the subsidy amount, the state regulations set the Bono Alquiler Joven at **250 euros per month** for a period of two years (up to 6,000 euros in total), provided the applicant meets income limits and rent caps [BOE-A-2022-802, Preámbulo, Artículo 8, Artículo 11].
