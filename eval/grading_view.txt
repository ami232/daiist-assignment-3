Q: What must the railway give me while I wait for a delayed train?
REF: For delays of more than 60 minutes: meals and refreshments in reasonable relation to the waiting time (if available), hotel or other accommodation and transport to it where a stay becomes necessary, and transport to the station or alternative transport if the train is blocked.
ANSWER: While you wait for a delayed train (amounting to 60 minutes or more, or if the service is cancelled), the railway undertaking must offer you the following free of charge, as soon as available:

* **Information:** You must be kept informed of the situation, the estimated departure time, and the estimated arrival time of the service or a replacement service [rail.pdf, Article 20, p. 21].
* **Meals and refreshments:** Meals and refreshments in reasonable relation to the waiting time, provided they are available on the train or in the station, or can reasonably be supplied [rail.pdf, Article 20, p. 21].
* **Accommodation:** Hotel or other accommodation, and transport between the railway station and the place of accommodation, if a stay of one or more nights becomes necessary or an additional stay becomes necessary, where and when physically possible [rail.pdf, Article 20, p. 21]. (The duration of accommodation may be limited to a maximum of three nights if the stay becomes necessary due to extraordinary circumstances not connected with the operation of the railway, extreme weather, major natural disasters, or major public health crises [rail.pdf, Article 20, p. 21, rail.pdf, Article 19, p. 20]).
* **Transport if blocked:** If the train is blocked on the track, transport from the train to the railway station, to the alternative departure point, or to the final destination, where and when physically possible [rail.pdf, Article 20, p. 21].
* **Alternative transport:** If the railway service is interrupted and cannot be continued anymore or within a reasonable period, the railway undertaking must provide passengers as soon as possible with alternative transport services and make the necessary arrangements [rail.pdf, Article 20, p. 21].

When applying these measures, the operating railway undertaking must pay particular attention to the needs of persons with disabilities and persons with reduced mobility, as well as any accompanying persons and assistance dogs [rail.pdf, Article 20, p. 21].

Q: How many bicycle spaces must new or upgraded passenger trains have?
REF: At least four bicycle spaces.
ANSWER: When initiating procurement procedures for new rolling stock, or when performing a major upgrade of existing rolling stock resulting in the need for a new vehicle authorisation, railway undertakings must ensure that train compositions are equipped with an "adequate number of places for bicycles" [rail.pdf, Article 6, pp. 12-13]. 

Railway undertakings determine this adequate number taking into consideration the size of the train composition, the type of service, and the demand for transport of bicycles, which is defined in specific plans [rail.pdf, Article 6, p. 13]. 

However, where there are no such plans or the plans do not determine such a number, each train composition must have at least four places for bicycles [rail.pdf, Article 6, p. 13]. Additionally, Member States may set a higher minimum number than four for certain types of services [rail.pdf, Article 6, p. 13]. 

This requirement does not apply to restaurant cars, sleeping cars, or couchette cars [rail.pdf, Article 6, p. 13].

Q: I was denied boarding on a 4000 km flight from Madrid against my will. What compensation and care am I entitled to?
REF: Compensation of EUR 600 plus the right to reimbursement or re-routing and to care (meals, refreshments, hotel if needed, communication).
ANSWER: If you are denied boarding against your will on a 4000 km flight, you are entitled to the following compensation, care, and assistance:

**Compensation**
- You are entitled to **EUR 600** compensation for flights not falling under 1,500 km or intra-Community flights of more than 1,500 kilometres (i.e., flights over 3,500 km) [air.pdf, Article 7, p. 5].
- This compensation can be reduced by 50% (to EUR 300) if you are offered re-routing to your final destination on an alternative flight whose arrival time does not exceed the scheduled arrival time of the originally booked flight by four hours [air.pdf, Article 7, p. 5].
- Compensation must be paid in cash, by electronic bank transfer, bank orders, or bank cheques, or—with your signed agreement—in travel vouchers and/or other services [air.pdf, Article 7, p. 5].

**Right to Reimbursement or Re-routing**
You must be offered your choice of:
1. **Reimbursement** of the full cost of the ticket at the price it was bought for the parts of the journey not made (and also for parts already made if the flight no longer serves a purpose in relation to your original travel plan), plus a return flight to the first point of departure at the earliest opportunity, where relevant [air.pdf, Article 8, p. 5].
2. **Re-routing** under comparable transport conditions to your final destination at the earliest opportunity [air.pdf, Article 8, p. 5].
3. **Re-routing** under comparable transport conditions to your final destination at a later date at your convenience, subject to seat availability [air.pdf, Article 8, p. 5].

**Right to Care**
You must be offered free of charge:
- **Meals and refreshments** in a reasonable relation to the waiting time [air.pdf, Article 9, p. 5].
- **Hotel accommodation** in cases where a stay of one or more nights becomes necessary, or where a stay additional to that intended becomes necessary [air.pdf, Article 9, p. 5].
- **Transport** between the airport and the place of accommodation (hotel or other) [air.pdf, Article 9, p. 5].
- **Two telephone calls, telex or fax messages, or e-mails** [air.pdf, Article 9, p. 5].
- Particular attention must be paid to the needs of persons with reduced mobility and any persons accompanying them, as well as unaccompanied children [air.pdf, Article 9, p. 5].
