## Caveats

- **Jurisdiction:** the questions don't say "Germany", and 17 of 39 baseline answers never mention German law (no German, BGB, BetrKV or Betriebskosten): q01, q04, q08, q09, q10, q13, q14, q16, q20, q22, q25, q30, q32, q33, q34, q37, q38. Many answer under UK or US law instead. Part of the gap therefore measures missing context, not missing knowledge. A user who adds "in Germany" would do better than this baseline.
- **Judge bias against the baseline:** on 3 answerable question(s) (q15, q25, q29) the judge failed the baseline partly because no passages were retrieved, treating it as if it had to refuse. Even if all of these flipped to correct, the baseline would be at 12/39.
- **Unanswerable questions:** JUDGE_PROMPT passes them only if the answer says the documents don't cover the question. The baseline has no documents, so it could pass only by declining. It never did (0/7 by the code check as well).
