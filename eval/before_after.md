# Before / after adding a document

Question q30: Within what period must the occupant of a home produce a title justifying their possession when the owner asks a court for immediate recovery of a squatted home?

## Before (base corpus only)

I don't know: the indexed laws (the Urban Leases Act, the Housing Law, and the Owners' Associations Act) do not cover the specific procedural deadlines for an occupant to justify their possession in a court action for the immediate recovery of a squatted home. 

That specific procedure is regulated by the Civil Procedure Law (*Ley de Enjuiciamiento Civil*, specifically the summary procedure for illegal squatting under Article 250.1.4º), which is not part of the indexed corpus. You would need to consult the Civil Procedure Law or a legal professional in Spain for the exact procedural deadlines.

## After (index includes 12 added chunks)

The occupant must produce a title justifying their possession within **5 days** (*cinco días*) from notification [L5/2018, Artículo único].

When the owner (or eligible claimant) requests immediate recovery of possession of a squatted home in their lawsuit, the court's decree admitting the lawsuit (*decreto de admisión de la demanda*) requires the occupants to provide a title justifying their possessory status (*título que justifique su situación posesoria*) within 5 days from receiving notice [L5/2018, Artículo único].

If the occupants fail to provide sufficient justification within that 5-day period, the court will issue an order (*auto*) directing the immediate delivery of possession of the property to the claimant, provided the claimant's documentation sufficiently proves their right to possess [L5/2018, Artículo único]. No appeal lies against this court order [L5/2018, Artículo único].

If you are facing or initiating court proceedings regarding illegal occupation, you should consult a qualified lawyer (*abogado*) or legal assistance office for guidance on your specific case.
