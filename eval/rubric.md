**correct** (1/0)

Score 1 when the answer states the same decisive fact as the reference answer: the verdict, number, deadline, threshold or named obligation. Wording and extra true detail don't matter.
Multi questions: the answer must cover both parts.
Score 0 when that fact is missing, wrong or contradicted, when the answer is hedged until it never commits, or when it answers a different question. A plain "I don't know" on an answerable question is also 0.
Unanswerable questions: score 1 only if the answer says the laws don't contain the information and doesn't state the missing fact as if it were established. Pointing to what the laws do say, such as the fine caps, is fine.

**supported** (1/0)

Score 1 when every factual claim can be found in the retrieved passages, directly or by plain paraphrase. Combining two retrieved statements is fine, and so is generic framing like "consult a lawyer".
Score 0 when any claim is missing from the passages, even if it is true. The judge checks only against the passages, not against the reference answer or its own knowledge.
Unanswerable questions: an answer that says the laws don't cover it and describes only what the passages say is 1.
Statements about what the corpus or the search results cover (e.g. "the corpus contains the AI Act, GDPR and DSA") are not factual claims about the law and do not count against support.
Partial credit: none. If a claim can't be found in the passages, it's 0.
